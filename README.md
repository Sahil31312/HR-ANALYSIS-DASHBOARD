# HR Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

This project presents an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built in Power BI to help organizations analyze workforce demographics, employee attrition, job satisfaction, and departmental performance.

The dashboard provides key HR metrics and visualizations that support data-driven workforce management and retention strategies.

---

## Key Metrics

* **Total Employees:** 1,416
* **Active Employees:** 1,000
* **Attrition Count:** 229
* **Attrition Rate:** 16.2%
* **Average Age:** 36.92 years
* **Average Experience:** 7.04 years

---

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Employee Overview

* Total employee count
* Active employee count
* Attrition statistics
* Workforce demographic summary

### 2. Attrition Analysis

* Attrition by department
* Attrition by salary slab
* Attrition by age group
* Attrition by gender
* Attrition trend by years at company

### 3. Department Analysis

* Employee distribution across departments
* Department-wise workforce count
* Department-specific attrition insights

### 4. Job Satisfaction Analysis

* Attrition percentage by job role
* Job satisfaction ratings (1–4 scale)
* Role-wise employee retention patterns

### 5. Demographic Insights

* Age group distribution
* Gender distribution
* Experience and tenure analysis

---

## Dashboard Insights

### Department-Wise Attrition

* Research & Development has the largest workforce.
* Sales department shows significant employee turnover.
* Human Resources has the smallest employee count.

### Salary Analysis

* Employees earning up to 5K salary range represent the largest workforce segment.
* Higher attrition is observed in lower salary brackets.

### Age Analysis

* Majority of employees belong to the 26–35 age group.
* Attrition is more concentrated among younger employees.

### Gender Analysis

* Male employees contribute a larger share of total attrition compared to female employees.

### Tenure Analysis

* Employee attrition is highest during the early years of employment.
* Attrition decreases as employee tenure increases.
